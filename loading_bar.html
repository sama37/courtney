Integrated Campaign

The Loading Bar

A campaign concept about slowing down, stepping away from constant digital noise, and making space for real human connection.

Year 2025
Role Campaign Strategy / Presentation Design
Type Brand Campaign
Client / Class College Collaborative Project
Tools PowerPoint • Illustrator • Photoshop

Project Overview

A campaign about being present again.

The Loading Bar is a campaign concept built around the idea that every connection is waiting to happen, like a loading bar slowly filling in a world consumed by screens, endless scrolling, and constant notifications.

The project creates a space for people to slow down, breathe, and reconnect through real human interaction in cozy, coffee shop inspired environments.

Through branding, posters, environmental design, and mockups, the campaign explores what it means to truly be present again and reminds people how meaningful face to face connection can be.

Working collaboratively as a team, we divided the campaign into different creative roles while making sure the final presentation felt visually cohesive and connected.

My contributions focused heavily on campaign strategy, developing the core message surrounding digital disconnection, and designing portions of the PowerPoint while ensuring the content matched the overall brand and atmosphere of the project.

The Challenge

Encouraging people to pause and reconnect.

The Loading Bar was created in response to the growing disconnect caused by technology and social media.

In a world filled with endless scrolling, notifications, and screen time, genuine face to face interaction has started to feel less common.

The challenge was to create a campaign that encouraged people to slow down, be present, and reconnect with others in a more meaningful way.

Instead of criticizing technology completely, the project focused on reminding people that real conversations, shared environments, and in person experiences still matter.

Through coffee shop inspired spaces, environmental branding, posters, and immersive visuals, the campaign created an atmosphere that felt warm, comforting, and intentionally human.

"Every connection is waiting to happen. You just have to pause long enough for it to load."

The Approach

Building a campaign around warmth and presence.

Our team approached the campaign by researching how constant digital consumption impacts attention spans, social interaction, and emotional presence.

A major insight that shaped the direction of the project was realizing how many people crave real human connection, yet still default to screens during moments of silence or discomfort.

We wanted to create an experience that felt calming instead of overwhelming, encouraging people to reconnect naturally rather than forcing interaction.

Visually, we leaned into cozy coffee shop inspired aesthetics, soft lighting, warm tones, and minimalist branding to contrast the overstimulating feeling of digital environments.

The loading bar symbol became the central visual element throughout the campaign, representing connection gradually building in real time.

Creative Execution

A visual identity designed around connection.

The final campaign included branded posters, environmental mockups, presentation slides, and experiential concepts designed around the theme of reconnecting through real human interaction.

The visual identity centered around clean typography, warm color palettes, loading inspired graphics, and cozy social spaces that encouraged people to pause and engage with one another.

Deliverables included poster designs, branded environmental installations, coffee shop themed mockups, and a polished presentation deck that brought the campaign concept together visually and strategically.

My role focused on helping guide the strategic direction of the campaign while also designing sections of the presentation deck to ensure the messaging and visual identity remained cohesive across all deliverables.

Final campaign deliverables included mockups and poster designs using loading bar visuals and messaging about human connection.

Outcomes

What the project achieved.

The Loading Bar successfully communicated a relatable issue surrounding digital disconnection while presenting a hopeful and emotionally grounded solution.

The project strengthened my understanding of campaign strategy, collaborative creative development, and designing with emotional storytelling in mind.

One of the biggest takeaways from this project was learning how branding and environmental design can influence the way people emotionally respond to a message.

Looking back, I would continue expanding the experiential side of the campaign by incorporating more interactive elements that encourage people to engage with strangers and disconnect from their devices in real time.
