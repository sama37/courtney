Social Media Design / Event Promotion

MSU MOTO Social Media Flyers

A collection of promotional flyers designed for the Michigan State University Motorcycle Club, with a featured case study on the Season Opener event poster.

Year 2025
Role Social Media Officer
Type Poster Series
Organization MSU MOTO
Tools Photoshop • Canva

Project Overview

Designing promotional content for MSU MOTO events and club engagement.

As the Social Media Officer for the Michigan State University Motorcycle Club (MSU MOTO), I created a series of promotional flyers and social media graphics designed to increase engagement, strengthen the club’s branding, and build excitement surrounding rides, events, and community activities.

These designs balanced high energy visuals with clear communication by combining dramatic imagery, layered typography, bold lighting effects, and organized event information.

My workflow typically involved building the main visual compositions in Adobe Photoshop before transferring the designs into Canva to finalize layout adjustments and optimize formatting for social media distribution.

Throughout the project series, I focused on maintaining a visually cohesive identity for the club while still allowing each flyer to feel unique and event specific.

Featured Design

Season Opener Campaign Flyer

One of the primary flyers from the series was the MSU MOTO Season Opener promotional design, created to capture the excitement surrounding the beginning of riding season while clearly communicating important event details and safety information.

Since the Season Opener is one of the club’s biggest events of the year, the flyer needed to feel visually impactful enough to build anticipation and encourage attendance from both returning members and new riders.

The overall goal was to create a design that reflected the club’s community driven atmosphere while strengthening MSU MOTO’s social media presence and visual identity online.

"The beginning of riding season should feel exciting before the ride even starts."

The Approach

Creating cinematic and high energy event promotion.

When designing the flyers, I focused on creating compositions that immediately felt immersive, energetic, and visually engaging while still remaining functional and easy to read.

I used dramatic lighting, layered typography, dropped shadows, glowing effects, and strong visual contrast throughout the designs to create depth and emphasize important information.

Michigan State University’s branding colors were incorporated throughout the flyers to maintain consistency with the club’s identity, while additional accent colors were often pulled directly from the motorcycles featured in the compositions to create stronger contrast and a more dynamic visual style.

After completing the main visual compositions in Photoshop, I finalized layout adjustments, event details, and social media formatting in Canva to prepare the graphics for online distribution.

Featured Season Opener flyer designed to build excitement around the beginning of riding season while communicating event information clearly through layered typography, dramatic lighting, and cinematic composition.

Flyer Collection

Additional promotional designs created for MSU MOTO.

Alongside the Season Opener flyer, I created additional promotional graphics for club announcements, events, and social media engagement throughout the riding season.

Each design focused on maintaining a consistent visual identity while adapting the composition, imagery, and typography to match the tone and purpose of each event.

Additional promotional flyers designed for MSU MOTO events, announcements, and community engagement throughout the riding season.

Outcomes

Strengthening engagement through visual identity and event promotion.

The flyer series successfully captured the high energy atmosphere surrounding MSU MOTO events while helping strengthen the organization’s visual identity across social media platforms.

The project allowed me to combine creative storytelling with practical event communication in a way that felt visually engaging and community focused.

One of the biggest takeaways from this experience was learning how strong visual hierarchy, branding consistency, and cinematic composition can improve audience engagement online.

The project also strengthened my skills in photo manipulation, typography, visual composition, and designing for both aesthetic impact and functional communication.
