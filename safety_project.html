Campaign Strategy / Experiential Design

Safety Quiz

A campaign concept designed to make pre flight safety information more interactive, rewarding, and memorable for airline passengers.

Year 2025
Role Campaign Strategy / Presentation Design
Type Campaign Concept
Concept Brand McDonald’s
Tools PowerPoint • Canva

Project Overview

Turning pre flight safety into something passengers want to pay attention to.

Safety Quiz is a campaign concept created to turn pre flight safety demonstrations into something passengers actually want to pay attention to.

The goal of the project was to create an experience that informed travelers about important airplane safety procedures in case of an emergency while making the process feel interactive, rewarding, and impossible to ignore.

To bring the idea to life, we partnered with McDonald’s and integrated their rewards program into the campaign. Passengers could scan a QR code, complete a short Safety Quiz before takeoff, and earn redeemable reward points and airport meal incentives.

We also created a limited edition plane shaped Layover Happy Meal to make the activation feel fun, collectible, and memorable while encouraging travelers to participate and share their experience.

Working collaboratively as a team, I contributed by leading the campaign strategy and helping design the PowerPoint presentation, ensuring that the visuals and content aligned with the playful, energetic branding throughout the project.

The Challenge

How do you make passengers care about safety instructions they usually ignore?

Safety Quiz was created to solve a problem that most airline passengers are familiar with: ignoring pre flight safety demonstrations.

Although airplane safety instructions are critical during emergencies, many travelers tune them out because the process feels repetitive, passive, and disconnected from the excitement of traveling.

The challenge was to transform an overlooked moment into an experience passengers would actively engage with and remember.

The campaign focused on making airline safety education feel interactive, rewarding, and impossible to ignore. By connecting the quiz to McDonald’s rewards, the experience gave passengers a clear incentive to participate before takeoff.

"When people actively participate in something, they’re far more likely to remember it."

The Approach

Using gamification, rewards, and playful branding to increase attention.

Our team began by researching why passengers often ignore pre flight safety instructions. One of the biggest insights we discovered was that travelers have become desensitized to traditional demonstrations because they feel repetitive and easy to dismiss.

Instead of using fear based messaging, we wanted to create a campaign that motivated attention through interaction, curiosity, and reward.

Working collaboratively as a team, we divided the project into different creative roles while ensuring the campaign remained visually cohesive and strategically connected.

My contributions focused heavily on leading the campaign strategy and helping design the PowerPoint presentation. I helped develop the core messaging around engagement and participation while making sure the visual identity reflected the playful, energetic tone of the campaign.

The overall design direction combined bold airline inspired visuals with recognizable McDonald’s branding to create an experience that felt exciting, familiar, and memorable.

Creative Execution

A playful campaign experience built around participation before takeoff.

The final campaign included branded posters, interactive quiz mockups, airport advertising concepts, presentation slides, and promotional visuals centered around the Safety Quiz experience.

Passengers would scan a QR code before takeoff, complete a short interactive quiz based on airplane safety procedures, and receive McDonald’s reward incentives through the app.

A major visual feature of the campaign was the limited edition airplane shaped Layover Happy Meal, which helped make the activation feel playful, memorable, and socially shareable.

The campaign branding combined bright colors, bold typography, and aviation inspired graphics to create a balance between entertainment and important safety communication.

My role focused on leading the strategic direction of the campaign while also helping design portions of the presentation deck to ensure the visuals, messaging, and branding remained cohesive throughout the final presentation.

Campaign deliverables included an interactive Safety Quiz interface, QR code activation mockup, Layover Happy Meal concept, branded posters, and airport promotional visuals.

Outcomes

Making safety education feel interactive, memorable, and shareable.

Safety Quiz successfully reframed airplane safety information as an interactive and rewarding experience rather than a passive announcement passengers could easily ignore.

The project strengthened my understanding of campaign strategy, gamification, brand partnerships, and designing for audience engagement.

One of the biggest takeaways from this project was learning how playful incentives and familiar branding can make important information feel more approachable without weakening the message.
